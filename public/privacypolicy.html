Aura health data privacy

Aura accesses health readings only after you choose to connect your device health platform and grant permission. You can deny or revoke any permission in your phone's health settings.

What Aura reads

With your permission, Aura reads heart rate, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, blood pressure, steps, and sleep records available from Apple Health or Health Connect. Availability depends on your device, companion app, and the permissions you grant.

How Aura uses the readings

Aura imports the selected readings into your signed-in Aura account so they can appear in your health history, trends, and analysis. The app does not write readings to Apple Health or Health Connect and does not share them with emergency contacts.

Storage and deletion

Imported readings are stored with the rest of your Aura health data under your account. You can delete health data or delete your account in Aura Settings. Deleting records from Aura does not delete the original readings from your phone's health platform.

Sync control

Sync happens only when you tap the sync action in Aura. Aura does not continuously monitor your readings in the background. Repeating a sync does not create duplicate records.
